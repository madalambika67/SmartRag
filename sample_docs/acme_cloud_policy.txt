Acme Cloud Hosting Handbook. Acme Cloud offers three plans: Starter at 9 dollars per month with 1 vCPU and 2 GB RAM, Pro at 29 dollars per month with 4 vCPU and 8 GB RAM, and Enterprise at 199 dollars per month with 32 vCPU and 128 GB RAM. All plans include daily backups retained for 14 days; Enterprise customers keep backups for 90 days.
Support policy. Starter customers receive email support with a 48 hour response time. Pro customers receive chat support with a 4 hour response time. Enterprise customers receive a dedicated engineer and a 30 minute response time for critical incidents.
Refund policy. Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of the first purchase. After 30 days, refunds are prorated only for outages exceeding the 99.9 percent uptime guarantee. Credits for downtime are applied automatically to the next invoice.
Security. All data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. Acme Cloud is SOC 2 Type II certified and performs penetration tests twice a year. Two-factor authentication is mandatory for all administrator accounts.
Migration. Acme provides free migration for Pro and Enterprise customers. The process has three steps: audit existing infrastructure, replicate data to Acme servers, and cut over DNS with less than five minutes of downtime.
